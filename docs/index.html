How fast are UK university homepages?

Google Lighthouse tests the homepage of each university every day, on mobile and desktop. Scores are the median of each site's latest week of tests. Tick universities to compare them side by side.

    Scores are Google Lighthouse lab results (0–100): 90 or more is good, 50–89 needs improvement, under 50 is poor. The 30-day change compares the mean performance score of the latest 30 days of tests with the 30 days before.

    Web Vitals lines are each divided by Google's "good" threshold (LCP 2.5 s, FCP 1.8 s, TBT 200 ms, CLS 0.1), so anything under the dashed line is good and lower is better. The scale is logarithmic, from a quarter of the threshold to 16×. Lines in this list are 7-day rolling medians to smooth out day-to-day noise; detail pages show every day.
